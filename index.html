My promise on safety

You have been told, quite rightly, to be careful about who you let near your computer. So here is exactly what I will never do:

  • I will never ask for your bank details or your card number.
  • I will never ring you out of the blue saying there's a problem with your computer. If someone does that, it's a scam — put the phone down.
  • I will never ask to take control of your computer from somewhere else. I work with you, in your home, where you can see the screen.
  • I will never ask you to pay before I've done the work.
  • Your passwords stay yours. Where I need one to fix something, you type it in, not me — and I'll tell you if anything ought to be changed afterwards.
